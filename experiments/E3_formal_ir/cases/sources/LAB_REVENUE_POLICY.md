# LAB revenue reporting policy (LAB_AUTHORED for Sophon E3; not a real policy)

Each clause is identified as [clause_id vVERSION]. Provenance: LAB_AUTHORED by the E3 orchestrator.

[S1 v1] Report, for each calendar year of order date, the revenue from completed orders, counting only order lines whose charged unit price is at least 100.

[S2 v1] Report, for each calendar year of order date, the revenue from completed orders that is attributable to the French market.

[S3 v1] Report, for each calendar year of order date, the number of distinct orders placed through the online channel by customers whose country of residence is France, regardless of order status.

[S4 v1] Report, for each calendar year of order date, the revenue from completed orders whose payment has been independently confirmed as settled by a payment processor.
