## A. Executive answer

Evidence against a standalone Sophon product, on the verified record, with the cheapest available route to falsify that call, which still requires NDA access to at least one importer's classification log.

1. No live review pool in any cluster was partitioned into missing facts versus interpretation. The two coded distributions that exist are disputed tails: IPSA's 46 appeal reviews (discretion or exception 18, rule meaning 12, evidence 2; IPSA committed to 4 of 17 recommended rule changes) (IPSA) and FOS overturn grounds (policy wording 16 of 626 upheld, a regex floor, 49% untagged) (FOS-BENCH). Where primary data partitions a residual by cause, it is input, evidence or per-item classification: SNAP FY2024 QC error dollars 77.7% household-side input, 4.6% pure computation; VA 42.5% of appealed issues remanded for evidence; SSA rates DDS determinations 97.1% policy-accurate in FY2022 (GOV-DATA). Prior authorisation: 7% of commercial medical claims face PA, 97 to 98% are approved, initial denials are "often a result of incomplete information" (AHIP, industry association). None of these is a why-humans-review distribution and none bounds H in a live pool.
2. Every institutional settlement loop found is run by the rule owner, not a software buyer, and is already versioned, published or executable: HMRC ATaR records each ruling with justification, search and exclusion method, effective dates, typed cancellation reasons and an appeal ladder (ATAR); the mirror holds 2,166 final EBA Q&As indexed by act, article and paragraph (EBA); Visa CE3.0 is a typed evidence object in Stripe's API (STRIPE); GSE DU and LPA, CMS-4201-F criteria, reinsurer manuals and Lloyd's binder endorsements are the same shape [RECALL].
3. Inside operating organisations two things must be kept apart. Per-entity settlements, category B, ship as typed expiring objects: Marble RuleSnooze with creator, source decision, rule, pivot, start and expiry (MARBLE); Elastic exceptions created from an alert (vendor docs, fetched). Class-level rule versioning also exists: Marble ScenarioIteration.Version (MARBLE); Avalara's effective-dated, user-stamped TaxRule, typed dispute and reclassification flags, a versioned classification mode and a write-back webhook on HSCodeAssigned (AVALARA, data model only); ServiceNow decision tables and exception records with sys_audit and retained retired document versions (SNOW). Only the second bears on H, and it shows the incumbents' data models already carry the executable half. The residual gap is narrow: no inspected incumbent records "settled by X on date D under power P as rule R version V applied prospectively". IBM ODM Decision Center, Pega and Drools Business Central could not be documented, so even the gap is unproven.
4. Wherever the settled object is an interpretation of text rather than a code, the institution keeps individual settlements non-precedential on purpose: the standard IRC 6110(k)(3) disclaimer in PLRs (PLR; quotation verified, not counted across the corpus), IPSA Scheme s.2.3 (IPSA), a clinician on every medical-necessity denial (AHIP, self-reported plan commitment), FOS fair-and-reasonable, DSA Art. 21(2), an ERISA named fiduciary on every appeal [RECALL for the last three]. PubLedge, the one open-source register for this artefact class, parked its product ambitions with 0 stars (PUBLEDGE); the settlement-loop verifier rates that as near-zero information about demand.

**What would overturn the call.** Four company-side pools were never observed because their data is not public: an importer's classification log, a PSP's underwriting queue, a bank's CDD exception log, a mortgage lender's help-desk log. Every verifier named one as the only thing worth testing and set the prior at a single-digit to low-teens H share. The structural examinations in section C each returned no data and structural arguments against. Section H specifies the one test worth running.

**Geography.** The direction holds in the US on the sourced record; for the EU and France it rests on recall plus three GitHub mirrors. The EU and France add human-oversight floors on adverse consumer decisions (GDPR Art. 22, AI Act Annex III) [RECALL]; France adds regulator-designed discretion budgets (the HCSF mortgage margin) [RECALL], rationed discretion, not ambiguity.

## C. Top three workflows

Each is carried as evidence against; they are the three where a company log could in principle be coded.

### C1. In-company customs tariff classification

**Triggering event.** A new SKU or part enters the item master, sourcing moves country, a supplier changes composition, an HTS or CN edition renumbers a line, or a broker, port officer or audit challenges a declared code. Avalara's data model describes the state machine: bulk jobs with counters classified, inProgress, notClassified, failed, notFound; a TariffCodeNeedsReview criterion; a request object with flags isReclassification, isDisputed, isPriority, isExportControl (AVALARA). The regulator-side analogue of a dispute is HMRC's LiabilityOrder with traderCommodityCode versus officerCommodityCode (ATAR).

**Available rules.** HS nomenclature with six General Interpretative Rules and section and chapter notes; national extensions (HTSUS 10-digit, CN 8-digit, TARIC 10-digit); explanatory notes; published rulings (CROSS 216,164; UK ATaR public search; EU EBTI blocked). Legal effect is recall throughout: a US ruling binds CBP only for the holder (19 CFR 177) [RECALL]; an EU BTI binds all Member State authorities and the holder for three years (UCC Art. 33) [RECALL]; France issues RTC through DGDDI under that regime [RECALL]; a UK ATaR is "valid from {0} to {1}" for the holder and third parties are told a similar ruling "can help" (ATAR).

**Available facts.** Description, composition, function, form, origin. Avalara's ItemModel carries parameters flagged isNeededForClassification, classifications per systemCode and country, created and modified user and date, and a premium free-text justification with user and date (AVALARA). HMRC's ruling office codes officer-to-officer referrals as REQUEST_SAMPLE and REQUEST_MORE_INFO with a NO_INFO_FROM_TRADER reject reason (ATAR): the vocabulary of one ruling office, not a rate, not an importer's queue. Trade-remedy note: 30.5% of 2023-24 rulings cite a chapter 99 measure (0% in 2017, before Section 301), and chapter 98 and 99 outcomes "depend on import circumstances, not product text" (VLADSWORK line 76), so that share is applicability on origin and facts (A, B), not interpretation.

**CROSS method note.** Year is ruling-date year. "Prior" means a ruling on the same line with a strictly earlier date; per-ruling prior counts take the maximum across cited codes. A 6-digit line is the first six digits of any cited code; a 10-digit line is the first ten digits of a code cited at ten digits, which drops the chapter 98 and 99 measure codes because they are cited at eight digits (1,356 of 1,363 such citations in 2023-24). Concentration and singleton statistics exclude chapters 98 and 99 explicitly. Supersession uses the modifies and revokes fields; lag is superseding minus superseded date. Same-day clusters group 2023-24 rulings by (ruling date, cited related ruling) and count memberships, so a ruling citing two related rulings can sit in two groups. Alternative definitions (the all-coded denominator for the 10-digit shares, or counting lines at eight digits) move the shares by up to five points and the median prior count between about 30 and 40; the script states each definition.

**Why it escalates.** Four observable reasons, none of them H by construction; H can only be identified in a company log. Missing facts (A). Per-item factual classification on settled lines (B): of the 3,247 rulings in 2023-24 citing any code, 98.8% sat on a 6-digit line that already had a ruling; of the 3,073 citing a 10-digit code, 93.9% sat on a 10-digit line that already had a ruling, the median such ruling sat on a line with 40 prior rulings, and 71.5% sat on lines with ten or more (CROSS; on the all-coded denominator the last two shares are 88.9% and 67.6%). Interpretation of text (C): 19.3% of 2023-24 rulings carry origin in the subject and 4.2% mention a set or kit (CROSS), proxies, not measures of interpretive content. Conflict (D): 1.41% of all rulings were ever modified or revoked, 99.1% of superseding rulings by HQ (CROSS). Caveat: CROSS is a reliance instrument requested on deeply settled lines and cannot estimate an in-company A/B/C/H split in either direction.

**Who resolves it.** Analyst, senior specialist, then the trade compliance director, who can set a family position and instruct brokers; beyond that a broker, counsel, or the authority through a ruling, which binds in the US only for the holder [RECALL]. The one reachable practice guide (ONURKAFK, weak) routes GRI 3 disputes, conflicting rulings and recurring or disputed classifications to professional review and a ruling request, which externalises the settlement.

**What exactly gets settled.** (1) The code for this item in this country, with dates and user (AVALARA). (2) A family reading such as "our brackets are parts of heading X": the H-shaped object. Avalara's model has no rule predicate, authority or applicability field, only itemGroup and category strings plus premium justification text; ONESOURCE, Descartes, SAP GTS and Oracle GTM are unverified. (3) The regulator's ruling with justification, methodSearch, methodCommercialDenomination, methodExclusion, effective dates, a CancelReason enum (INVALIDATED_CODE_CHANGE and others) and an appeal ladder from ADR to Supreme Court (ATAR).

**Prospective.** Item level, yes: TaxRule is effective-dated and user-stamped (AVALARA). Family level: a team convention with no sourced versioned object. Everyone-binding: 29, 26 and 31 HQ superseding rulings in 2022, 2023 and 2024, median lag 3.68 years, and 2023-24 HQ output dominated by protests (129 of 416 mention protest) (CROSS). 18.2% of the 3,971 rulings in 2023-24 carry no code (origin, carriers, entry) (CROSS), so "outcome is a code" holds for about four fifths of decisions.

**Recurrence.** Sourced at nomenclature level, unknown in-company. Excluding chapters 98 and 99, 85 six-digit lines of 1,006 cover half of the 3,723 line mentions in 2023-24 (one mention per ruling per line); 62.2% of 1,512 distinct 10-digit lines appear once in the two years (CROSS). Same-day co-citation clusters: 49 groups holding 135 memberships at two or more (3.4% of 3,971) and 22 groups holding 81 at three or more (2.0%) (CROSS). These are a batch-filing proxy, for example N341630 to N341634 on 2024-08-20 all citing 556449, one requester on one day, not an interpretive question recurring over time. Interpretive recurrence over time was not measured. The multi-country multiplier applies to codes stored, not ambiguities settled, because the six-digit core is shared [RECALL].

**Current systems.** ERP item master, GTM suites, broker systems, CROSS, EBTI and ATaR lookup; Avalara's Classification MCP server describes managed classification requests (AVALARA, vendor documentation). LLM classifiers grounded in CROSS reach 73.0, 59.6, 44.9, 38.2 and 32.6% at 2 to 10 digits on 89 held-out rulings (VLADSWORK, anecdotal).

**Minimal Sophon product.** A settlement register beside the item master recording, per escalated decision, the facts relied on, the rule reading, the authority cited, the decider and their power, effective dates and an applicability predicate; matching new SKUs on attributes; invalidating on typed triggers; exporting the audit trail. It would not classify, extract facts, decide chapter 99 or origin, file rulings or replace the GTM. The one inspected incumbent's data model already carries dispute and reclassification flags, a review-queue disposition endpoint, a versioned classification mode (AutoFull or AutoPartial with settingsVersion), a write-back webhook on HSCodeAssigned, per-item duty detail by origin including AD/CVD rates, HS verification batches and an slaInHours field on the classification output (AVALARA; field existence, no operational data). The residual is the rationale schema.

**Buyer.** Head of Global Trade Compliance or Customs Manager at importers with thousands of SKUs across two or more regimes; brokers secondary. Whether the classification labour sits in-house, with a broker or with a vendor's managed service is unknown and is the first interview question.

**ROI logic.** This report's own model on unsourced inputs, not evidence. 5,000 new SKUs a year, 10% escalated, 2 hours per escalation at 60 USD, H at 5 to 15%, 1.5 hours saved per H case: 2,250 to 6,750 USD a year; at ten times the flow, 22,500 to 67,500 USD. Labour cannot carry a product on those inputs. The error-cost case (a 10 million USD family across a 5-point differential is 500,000 USD a year) rests on two unsourced premises, and its inconsistency premise is contradicted by the like-item verification batches in the incumbent's data model (AVALARA). Verdict: evidence against; survives only as the cheapest place to run section H.

### C2. Merchant onboarding restricted-business classification at a PSP or acquirer

**Triggering event.** An application, periodic re-review or website-monitoring alert fails to resolve automatically because the business model is not cleanly inside or outside the restricted list or its MCC is contestable. In Stripe's API the account-level requirements.disabled_reason takes under_review, rejected.listed, rejected.terms_of_service or rejected.other, and a capability takes pending.review or rejected.unsupported_business (STRIPE). Adyen's capability verificationStatus goes pending then valid, invalid or rejected, "rejected" meaning Adyen "found reasons to not allow the capability" (ADYEN). Ballerine's case enters Manual review with Approve, Reject or Ask for all re-uploads (BALLERINE).

**Available rules.** The PSP's prohibited and restricted list, versioned [RECALL]; card-network rules on MCC accuracy, brand-risk registration and MATCH [RECALL]; the 981-code MCC space (MCC-CODES); Adyen types restricted industries as capabilities such as acceptTransactionInRestrictedIndustries (ADYEN); Ballerine's demo workflow gates documents on MCC (5499 and 5912 cannabis licence, 7800 and 7801 gambling licence, 5941 firearms authorisation) and its docs say "you can set a rule on an MCC code to auto-approve, auto-reject, or move to manual review" (BALLERINE, vendor docs, demo file, repo unsupported). Local overlays: state-legal cannabis, EU and French gambling licensing (ANJ) [RECALL].

**Available facts.** business_type (4 values), company.structure (23 values), free-string MCC, product_description "used by Stripe for risk and underwriting purposes", URL, owners (STRIPE). Ballerine's merchant report emits mcc, lineOfBusiness and typed risk indicators with reason, quote, source URL and screenshot, including a PROHIBITED_CONTENT violation id (BALLERINE). Missing licences and inaccessible websites are the routine gap [RECALL].

**Why it escalates.** Missing evidence (A) and factual classification of what is sold (B) are the structurally dominant causes; no queue was observed. Stripe's 96 requirement error codes partition as 30 document defects, 16 URL defects, 23 identity, ownership and tax-id mismatches, 19 format and value codes, 5 denylist matches and 3 other (external_request, information_missing, unsupported_business_type) (STRIPE). This enum reports KYC data defects to the platform; restricted-business outcomes surface as disabled_reason values, so it says nothing about the share of underwriter reviews that are interpretive. Interpretive residue (C, D, H) is whether a hemp subscription is "CBD" or a sweepstakes model is "gambling" under the list although lawful locally. Adyen's VerificationError type enum is invalidInput, dataMissing, pendingStatus, rejected, dataReview (ADYEN).

**Who resolves it.** L1 underwriter; senior underwriter; head of merchant risk or a risk committee with the MLRO for list changes. Rule-making power exists only for principal-member acquirers and PSPs underwriting on their own licence; payfacs and platforms are underwritten by their PSP or sponsor, the structure the Stripe and Adyen APIs express [sponsor-bank binding is RECALL]. Network brand-risk programmes keep a per-merchant registration step for the verticals where ambiguity sits [RECALL].

**What exactly gets settled.** A class-level outcome: "models with features X and Y are list category Z, acceptable with conditions, from date D". **Recurrence.** Unknown; no analogue transfers. **Current systems.** In-house onboarding and underwriting tools exposing typed state (STRIPE, ADYEN); Ballerine (unsupported open-source repo); Middesk, Alloy, Persona, LegitScript, G2, Web Shield [RECALL].

## D. Best initial wedge

The historical classification log at one importer that classifies in-house, US first (HTSUS, CROSS), with one UK or EU importer among the discovery targets (ATaR or BTI; France as RTC). Not a market-size argument: it is the cheapest available place to get a real answer, it still needs NDA access to a confidential log, and the honest prior is that the answer kills it.

**Why this workflow.** (1) At the one inspected vendor the corpus exists in structured form: ItemModel and ItemHSCodeClassificationStatusModel carry item code, description, item group, classifications per country, created and modified user and date, status, details and completedAt (AVALARA, data model); ONESOURCE, Descartes, SAP GTS and Oracle GTM are unverified, so this is a fact about one vendor, not the class. (2) A public precedent corpus with dates and supersession links is reachable for the external check (CROSS; ATaR public search). (3) The decision owner holds rule-making power over the company's own filings, not over heading meaning, which belongs to CBP, HMRC or the EU: a partial pass, better than mortgage (GSE-owned), claims (courts and FOS) or reporting (EBA). (4) Decisions are classifications and approvals, not adverse decisions about individuals, so the H4 constraints do not apply. (5) The class key is anchored by a public taxonomy. (6) The outcome is a code plus dates, executable in the item master, for about four fifths of public decisions (CROSS). (7) The incumbent gap is documented at Avalara only: no rationale on TaxRule, justification only as premium free text, no family rule or applicability object (AVALARA).

**Why not the larger markets.** Mortgage: the lender cannot make guide readings authoritative, the owners encode the largest interpretive area, the log is email with NPI, and on this report's own model the desk is sub-FTE. Merchant onboarding: in-house tooling, no export pattern examined, PSP access harder than importer access, the model fails H8. KYC/CDD: hundreds to low thousands of exceptions a year (guess), a rationale per exception, CLM vendors own the rules. P&C claims: settled object owned externally, wording 2.6% of upheld (FOS-BENCH, weak). Regulatory reporting: EBA owns the meaning and runs the store free. Lloyd's DA: a single interview, not an experiment.

**Access to historical cases.** Item-master export; the classification worksheet; escalation emails and broker requests for 12 months; broker instruction letters; post-entry corrections, protests and reconciliation adjustments; the classification manual with version history; ruling requests. Brokers hold worksheets for many importers, but those are the importer's confidential records needing that importer's consent (19 CFR 111.24) [RECALL], and brokers earn per-line fees [RECALL], so brokers are referral and cost-anchor sources, not the primary log.

**Access to decision owners.** Director of Global Trade Compliance, Customs Compliance Manager, Classification Lead; reachable by title and through NCBFAA, ICPA and AAEI [RECALL as channels]. Two questions gate the experiment in the first interview: does the company classify in-house, through vendor managed classification or through a broker; and does the compliance lead ever issue a standing family rule analysts must apply. A no to either ends the wedge before any log is coded.

## E. Competitive threat

**Shape:** a feature inside an existing workflow product, specifically the item-master and GTM owner, on the evidence of the one GTM data model inspected. Second: a consulting or service layer, since classification is already sold per line by brokers [RECALL] and described as a managed service by Avalara (AVALARA, vendor documentation). "Not built at all" is the live outcome if the base-rate experiment returns single-digit H, which is the prior. Standalone survives only if H clears the economic gate at companies using several GTM systems or none and the vendors decline to add the fields.

**Reasoning.** The unshipped piece is a field set and a link, not a system. ServiceNow and Marble carry the same increment in their domains (SNOW, MARBLE). Every institution that runs a settlement loop built it itself (ATAR, CROSS, EBA, STRIPE for CE3.0). Who would absorb it: Avalara, ONESOURCE, Descartes, E2open, SAP GTS, Oracle GTM (feature sets unverified except Avalara's data model); brokers and forwarder brokerage arms as a service layer; the authorities on the public side (HMRC ships the loop as open source and is building AI drafting with review and provenance, per its repos); AWS AgentCore and Microsoft AGT if framed as a generic control plane.

## H. Base-rate experiment

This design incorporates the methodology review in `evidence/stage2/review_design.json`, which refuted the original gates 2, 3 and 5 as vacuous or uncomputable, rejected the assumption that a non-specialist coder could code tariff cases, and ruled out broker records without the importer's consent. Gates are pre-registered before coding and not revised once shares are known.

**Sample.** 300 to 500 classification decisions per company from one to three companies, at least one an in-house importer team, from a single 12-month window, with a further 12 months for recurrence to avoid censoring. Two strata: (i) a random 150 to 200 from all decisions, for the all-decision share of categories 7 and 8; (ii) all escalated decisions if 300 or fewer, otherwise a random 200 to 300, stratified so no family exceeds 25%. If escalations cannot be identified, use a stated proxy and report it (completedAt minus createdAt over one working day; modifiedUserId differing from createdUserId; the vendor needs-review status), noting each is confounded and cross-company comparisons on different proxies are invalid. Minimum stratum: about 100 identifiable escalations a year; below that H8 is answered by volume and the experiment is not run. Precision: at n = 400 and a true share of 15% the 95% interval is about plus or minus 3.5 points; at n = 200 per stratum about plus or minus 5, enough to separate 8% from 15% from 25%.

**Source of cases.** The item-master export joined to the worksheet, escalation emails, broker requests and instruction letters, the manual with versions, ruling requests, and the correction and protest log. Per case a decision packet: description and spec available at decision time, code per country, who and when, whether a prior same-family item existed at that date and its code, whether a public ruling on the same line existed (full-text lookup; the CROSS CSV carries no ruling text, so use CBP's search or the ATaR public search), escalation status and reason, later corrections, and the duty-rate differential between candidate codes. The packet records which elements of the HMRC Decision record (justification, methodSearch, methodCommercialDenomination, methodExclusion, effective dates, cancellation) the company record holds (ATAR), yielding a per-case record-sufficiency score as a pre-registered secondary outcome.

**Zero-coder metric,** computed first from the export: items in the same group or with near-identical descriptions carrying different codes for the same country; the same item coded differently across entities or brokers; items recoded after a correction. The first signal of capture failure, at no cost.

**Coding protocol.** Two coders with tariff competence (a licensed broker or former classification specialist, and a former customs officer or trade counsel), independent of the company and of each other, blind to the hypothesis and the interviews. A third, non-specialist pass on a 60-case subsample tests whether the codebook carries the categories; descriptive, not a gate. Each coder assigns one primary and at most one secondary category plus a class key through an ordered tree. Q1: was a schedule-required fact absent, causing a request, hold or delay? Yes: primary 2. Q2: with facts on file, could the coder reproduce the code in under five minutes from the notes and an existing template or prior same-family decision, with no judgment call? Yes: primary 1. Q3: was the work determining what the product is under a rule whose meaning was not in dispute, such that another product in the family needs the same per-item work? Yes: primary 3. Q4: did the decision turn on what a rule text means? No: go to Q7. Yes: Q5: had the company settled that question for the same family before this date, evidenced by a citable dated record? Yes: primary 7 (record whether re-decided or contradicted). No: Q6: does the record show an authorised person stating the reading in family-general terms intended to govern future items? Yes: primary 8; if the reading exists only in this item's record: primary 5. Q7: did two authorities or instructions point to different codes and the decision resolved which prevails? Yes: primary 6. Q8: did the company choose among defensible codes on risk or commercial grounds for this shipment with no intent to govern other items? Yes: primary 4. Residue: 3, flagged. Because Q1 runs first, cases with both a facts request and an interpretive question fall to 2; the primary-or-secondary H share is reported alongside the primary-only share, and the gate uses primary-only as pre-registered. Class key for 7 and 8: the 6-digit line or family times the interpretive question type. HMRC's reject reasons ATAR_RULING_ALREADY_EXISTS and DUPLICATE_APPLICATION (ATAR) are the worked analogue of category 7. For broker records the authorised person for category 8 is the client's compliance lead, never the broker.

**Category definitions.** (1) Fully deterministic: facts on file plus the GIRs, notes and an existing template or prior same-family decision yield one code with no judgment; a prior settlement found and applied without re-deciding is coded 1 with its class key retained. (2) Missing evidence: blocked or delayed because composition, fibre content, function, material breakdown, sample or spec was absent. (3) Factual classification: facts available, but essential character, principal function or material predominance required specialist reading under a rule whose meaning was not in question. (4) One-off discretion: a defensible code chosen on risk or commercial grounds for this shipment with no intent to govern other items. (5) Ambiguity of rule meaning left case-specific: the decision turned on rule text and the record states no family-level reading. (6) Conflict: two rulings, jurisdictions, instructions or templates pointed to different codes and the decision resolved which prevails. (7) Recurring previously-settled ambiguity: settled by the company for the same family before this date, evidenced by a citable dated record, and a human re-decided it or decided inconsistently. (8) New ambiguity settled prospectively: the question was new and an authorised person stated the reading in family-general terms intended to govern future items.

**Agreement.** Pilot on 50 to 60 escalation-stratum cases; gate on the binary (7 or 8 versus other) at Cohen's kappa of at least 0.75, eight-way kappa reported descriptively. One codebook revision allowed, then a fresh pilot; a second failure stops the experiment and is reported as a finding that the records do not separate facts from interpretation, itself evidence against the product. Full sample: percentage agreement, both kappas, each coder's shares and adjudicated shares. Adjudicator: the company's compliance lead, blind to which coder gave which code, who also states per category-8 case whether they would ratify the reading. Gates apply to the lower coder's pre-adjudication share; thin records under-count category 7, so H is also reported conditional on the record-sufficiency score.

**Thresholds, set by customer economics.** All parameters are estimates until interviews replace them, and gates are recomputed with interview values before scoring. Model: L = N times C (escalations per year times loaded cost per escalation); S = h7 times r times L plus h8 times u times r times L minus capture cost, where h7 and h8 are the category shares, r the fraction of an escalation's cost a settled reading removes, u the within-year reuse rate of a new settlement, and capture cost about one hour per settlement (estimate). Requirement: S at least 3P in year one, P the annual price, because a mid-market buyer will not add a point tool beside a GTM suite on less than roughly three-to-one verifiable labour savings, and risk avoided is not verifiable at sale (heuristic). Central values: C 200 USD (100 to 300), N 2,500 (500 to 5,000), r 0.5, u 0.5, P 30,000 USD. Required h at the central case: 90,000 divided by (0.5 times 2,500 times 200) is 36%, illustrative until interviews supply C and N, and above every H estimate in this project. At N 5,000 and C 300 it is 12%; at N 10,000 and C 300, 6%.

**Gates.** Gate 1, frequency: categories 7 plus 8 at least the required h from the interview-supplied L, 36% at central estimates and 15% only where L reaches about 1.2 million USD; and at least 5% of the all-decision stratum. The 15% figure is also the payments verifier's kill line, a tail value, not a default. Gate 2, recurrence: on coded cases assigned an interpretive class over the 24-month window, at least 50% of category 7 plus 8 cases fall in classes with three or more coded occurrences and no more than 40% of classes are singletons; line or family recurrence is reported separately as factual recurrence. Basis: break-even is one reuse at r times C of 100 USD against 100 USD of capture cost, compounding needs a median of three, and the owner's permission log (47.5% of settlements never reused, median two later calls, private data) is the prior for failure. Gate 3, capture failure: the re-decision rate where a prior settlement existed, 7 divided by (7 plus 1-with-class-key), at least 50%; if prior settlements were mostly found and applied, H3 holds. Gate 4, ratification: at least one third of category-8 cases ratified by the owner in family-general terms within the window. Gate 5, public duplication: fewer than 50% of category 7 plus 8 settlements match a public ruling on the same question at the decision date, judged on ruling text, not line match, since roughly 93 to 98% of decisions will match a prior ruling on the same line if the company's items resemble the public ruling population (CROSS; untested transfer); if it fails, the remedy is retrieval over the public corpus, not a private ledger. Gate 6, records: no more than 20% of sampled cases below the record-sufficiency floor.

**Shadow test.** Four to eight weeks of prospective checking in which each new escalation is compared against the captured settlements, counting hits and whether the specialist would have applied the settlement. Retrospective coding measures recurrence and capture failure, not a tool's effect; this is the cheapest first reading of that effect.

**Decision rule.** Continue toward a product only if all six gates pass at two or more companies, or at one company whose measured L times h clears the payback requirement with interview-supplied C and N, and the GTM vendor interview did not show the fields already exist. Pivot to a feature partnership or a broker-run service if h sits between 8% and the required gate, if gates 1 to 4 pass in only one company, or if gate 5 fails. Stop if h is below 8% everywhere, gate 2 or 3 fails everywhere, the pilot cannot reach the kappa gate after one revision, or no company exports a log within the sprint. Publish coder-level shares with intervals, gate values with parameters, and the category 7 and 8 classes with recurrence counts.

**What it cannot decide.** Willingness to pay, budget line, ACV or sales cycle. Whether ONESOURCE, Descartes, SAP GTS or Oracle GTM already carry the objects. Generalisation beyond the sampled companies (H5), whose willingness to share biases the sample in an unknown direction. Whether a captured settlement would be found at the next decision, beyond the shadow test. Cost per escalation and escalations per year, which are self-reported. Whether settled readings are right in law; a consistently applied wrong code compounds exposure under reasonable-care obligations [RECALL]. Durability beyond the window. The boundary between categories 3 and 5 where the fact is itself a legal construct such as essential character; rule order treats it by convention. Market size or the number of companies above the L threshold.

## Hypotheses H1 to H8

## Closing

**I would stop searching for a standalone Sophon product because every institution examined that runs a settlement loop built it itself and keeps individual settlements non-precedential on purpose, because the operating organisations that would buy already hold the executable half inside their case manager, rules engine or item master, and because no live review pool was measured for repeatable settleable ambiguity anywhere, while every verifier's estimate for the unobserved company-side pools sits in the single digits to low teens and the one gate computed from plausible customer economics needs roughly 36% at central estimates.**
