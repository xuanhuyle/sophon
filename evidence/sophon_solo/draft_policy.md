# Sophon Lab expense policy — DRAFT, NOT ADOPTED

This fictional organization is a test sandbox. Its policy has **no authority** until the experiment owner explicitly adopts a version and an effective date. IPSA is used only as inspiration for adversarial case shapes; IPSA rules and past decisions do not govern this organization.

1. Reimbursement requires a documented company purpose and supporting evidence. An employee may not approve their own expense.
2. Hotel costs for necessary business travel should be **reasonable** for the destination and circumstances. A manager must approve travel.
3. Meeting refreshments should be **modest** and serve a legitimate work purpose. Alcohol and tips are not reimbursable. Meals celebrating a team achievement require case-specific approval by the policy owner.
4. Equipment should be obtained through the shared IT catalogue **where practicable**. A different supplier requires documented unavailability or business necessity.
5. Claims exceeding £1,000 require Finance approval; claims exceeding £5,000 require CFO approval. These are strict lower bounds: £1,000 itself does not trigger the first threshold.
6. Only the policy owner can create a bounded exception or change the policy. A case-specific approval identifies the beneficiary, action, amount or cap, purpose and effective period. It does not authorize later similar claims.
7. An interpretation of an open term, a new constraint, a relaxation, a one-off exception and a source amendment are different acts. The system must display their different effects for review; the actor must have power to perform the chosen act.
8. A newly ratified interpretation applies from its declared effective time. An amendment must identify whether earlier interpretations survive; unresolved dependencies suspend automatic clearance until reviewed. An earlier payment decision alone never ratifies a prospective interpretation.

## Open issues requiring a real sandbox-owner settlement

- What operational bounds, if any, define a reasonable hotel price by location, event and season?
- What makes refreshments modest? Does a restaurant meal after a work session count?
- What evidence demonstrates that the IT catalogue is impracticable?
- Who has delegated authority to approve a specific exception, beyond the owner?

The owner may choose to retain semantic review rather than impose a threshold. The exercise must not optimize for a target clearance rate by inventing permissions.
