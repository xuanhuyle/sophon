# Solo experiment protocol, pre-registration v0

## Objective and claims allowed

Test whether an explicit, owner-ratified settlement can be carried through a versioned authorization runtime, reused on later fully specified cases, and safely invalidated. This controlled dataset is synthetic by design and **cannot** estimate real-world case frequency, human governance savings in a company, or IPSA compliance.

## Inputs already frozen

- `draft_policy.md`: authoring proposal, not yet adopted.
- `cases.csv`: 48 synthetic, label-free, chronological expense requests in four recurring families. A source-and-data SHA-256 manifest is in `manifest.json`.
- `../sophon_cases/cases.json`: five real published reviews that inspire adversarial boundary tests. They are *not* the policy of Sophon Lab.

## Necessary human step

The experiment owner explicitly adopts or edits the policy with an effective time. Every later settlement is recorded as `INTERPRET_OPEN_STANDARD`, `ADD_CONSTRAINT`, `RELAX_CONSTRAINT`, `CASE_EXCEPTION`, or `AMEND_SOURCE` with issuer, bounded power, targeted clause/version, reason, precise scope, effective time, and any expiry. A case decision cannot be promoted to a rule simply by clicking “approve”. Drafts generated by a model have no normative force. The owner times their intervention from first viewing the proposal until recording a decision; reviewing, editing and rejected proposals count.

## Replay

1. Establish a baseline by manually writing deterministic rules against the same adopted policy and case schema. A coding assistant may help, but its effort and the owner's review time must be logged. The baseline cannot use historical outcomes or future settlements.
2. Run the Sophon arm chronologically. The first 24 cases are the settlement period. For each unresolved case, record the state before any settlement; ask the owner whether to decide only this case, ratify a reusable interpretation, amend policy or leave unresolved. A proposed interpretation must not rewrite prior cases.
3. Freeze all authority state at the boundary between cases 24 and 25. Score cases 25–48 without further executable settlements. This is a deliberately favorable repetition test: it measures *mechanical reuse*, not how often real expenses repeat.
4. After the frozen score, perform a **separate** owner-authorized amendment on one open standard and one case-specific exception. Replay affected decisions prospectively; list invalidated dependencies. Never rewrite historical decisions.
5. Feed five published-review case shapes into adversarial tests: a named grant after expiry, a non-precedential 50% remedy, a proposed guidance clarification, a rule change between conduct and review, and separate line items with different funding sources. These are fixture tests of authority provenance only; no IPSA reimbursement is authorized.

## Metrics and interpretation

- Count full `CLEAR`, `BLOCK`, `REQUEST_FACT`, `REQUEST_AUTHORITY`, `SEMANTIC_REVIEW`, and `CONFLICT` by case and by policy version. `CLEAR` requires all facts, adequate authority, a bound exact amount and any required approvals.
- For each ratified interpretation, count *later frozen cases* it affects and the cases it fully decides **because of that interpretation**, net of other blockers. Report both; matching a predicate is not equivalent to clearance.
- Report owner minutes per attempted normative act, including rejected and revised proposals, and separate implementation/developer minutes for both arms.
- Report zero false `CLEAR` only against independent owner-adjudicated case labels produced *after the owner has adopted the policy*; otherwise report safety tests, not an accuracy percentage.
- Compare with a policy-as-code baseline at equal deterministic coverage. Given the small designed sample and one participant, report descriptive minutes and learning effects; no claim of statistically generalizable economic advantage.

## Stop conditions

Stop a run and repair the mechanism if a proposal becomes operative without the owner's ratification; a case-only remedy spreads; an expired exception passes; a revoked or superseded act remains executable; a later policy is applied retrospectively without explicit authority; a missing fact silently becomes true; or an authorized decision is reused for a different amount, beneficiary or effect.

## Stage boundary

The research program can produce a credible **architectural decision and runnable vertical slice** alone. It cannot validate organization-level governance economics or customer demand without subsequent operational evidence. The prompt to Claude Code asks for the former, with the latter expressly unresolved.
